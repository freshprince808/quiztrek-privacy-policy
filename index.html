Information We Collect

QuizTrav stores learning progress, settings, achievements, saved phrases, flashcards, and classroom data locally on your device. No personal information is transmitted to external servers during normal use.

QuizTrav does not sell personal information.

Offline Functionality

Most QuizTrav features operate completely offline and do not require an internet connection. No account, sign-up, or login is required to use the app.

Data Storage

All learning data is stored locally on your device using secure device storage. No data is stored on our servers.

Third-Party Services

QuizTrav may use platform services provided by Apple, Google, or Expo for app distribution, crash reporting, or app updates where applicable. If you choose to enable the optional AI language assistant (School Edition), lesson prompts are sent to your own BYOK (Bring Your Own Key) AI provider over HTTPS. No data is shared with our servers.

Children's Privacy

QuizTrav is intended for educational use and is rated for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. No account creation, ads, or external links are accessible to children during normal gameplay.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect user information and application data. All data remains on your device and is subject to your device's built-in security.

Future Features

Future features such as cloud synchronization may require additional permissions. Any such changes will be reflected in an updated Privacy Policy posted on this page.

Contact
