Frequently Asked Questions

Is QuizTrav free?

Yes. All current features — quizzes, Language Worlds, Travel Intelligence, Classroom Mode, and more — are free to use. No subscription or purchase is required.

Does QuizTrav require an internet connection?

No. QuizTrav is fully offline. All content, progress, flashcards, and settings are stored on your device. No internet connection is required for any core feature.

How do I reset my progress?

Go to Settings → Advanced → Reset Progress. This clears your XP, achievements, and saved data. This action cannot be undone.

How do I delete my data?

All data is stored locally on your device. To delete it, go to Settings → Advanced → Reset Progress, or uninstall the app. No data is stored on our servers.

The Lens Explorer camera is not working.

Make sure you have granted camera permission to QuizTrav in your device Settings. On iOS: Settings → QuizTrav → Camera → Allow. On Android: Settings → Apps → QuizTrav → Permissions → Camera → Allow.

I found a bug or crash.

Please email area1985@hotmail.com with: your device model, iOS/Android version, app version (visible in Settings), and a description of what happened. Screenshots or screen recordings are very helpful.
